# Readfirst

## What it is
A desktop app for founders and small sales teams who send cold email themselves. It finds or imports leads, drafts personal emails, sends from the person's own mailbox and sorts replies. Nothing leaves until the person has read and approved it, and spending never passes their limit.

## Who uses it
A founder or solo seller, not a trained marketer, working alone at a laptop between other jobs. First-time users do not know terms like SPF, IMAP or suppression list. They open it a few times a day to approve drafts and answer replies.

## What success looks like
Someone with no experience gets from a fresh install to a first approved email without help, and always knows the next step.

## Register
Product: Operate. The public site is Persuade.

## Must stay true
Approval before sending. Budget limits. Opt-out in every email. Keyboard, screen reader and phone width support. No invented claims, numbers or testimonials.

## The owner's taste
Dislikes neon, glassmorphism and trendy AI-generated looks. Called the current app ugly, plain, hard, confusing and boring. Wants it real, product-led, exclusive and easy.
